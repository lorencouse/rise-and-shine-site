Rise and Shine

Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Rise and Shine does not collect any data about you. Everything the app knows stays on your devices. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What the app uses, and where it stays

Location

Your location is used only to calculate local sunrise and dawn times, and the time zone your alarm should ring in. It is stored on your device.

To turn coordinates into a city name, the app uses Apple's map service. Apple receives the coordinates for that lookup under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive them.

If you turn on Update alarms when I travel, iOS lets the app know when you've moved a significant distance, even while it isn't open, so your alarm can follow the local sunrise. This uses low-power location changes and needs "Always" location access. You can turn it off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Health (optional)

If you allow it, the app reads your sleep data from Apple Health to compare last night's sleep with your sleep goal. It only reads; it never writes anything to Health.

Health data stays on your device. It is not used for advertising or marketing, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone.

Calendar (optional)

If you turn on calendar sync, the app adds "Sleep" events to its own "Rise and Shine" calendar and keeps them up to date. It does not read your other calendars' events. Turning the option off removes those events.

Notifications and alarms

Alarms are scheduled with iOS's alarm system, and bedtime reminders are scheduled as local notifications. Both are created on your device; nothing is sent to a server.

Apple Watch and iCloud

Your settings are sent to your paired Apple Watch so it can show your next alarm. Your settings may also sync to your other devices through your own iCloud account, which is handled by Apple. We cannot see that data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no servers, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no advertising. We do not sell or share data, because we never receive any.

Deleting your data

Use Reset all settings in the app's Settings, or delete the app. To revoke Location, Health or Calendar access, go to iOS Settings › Privacy & Security.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
