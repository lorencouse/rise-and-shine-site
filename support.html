Support

Need help or found a bug? Email cousethemouse@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a couple of days.

Common questions

My alarm didn't ring.

Check that alarms are allowed in iOS Settings › Rise and Shine, and that the day isn't skipped or turned off on the Home screen. Alarms ring through Silent mode and Focus, just like the Clock app.

The alarm time changes every day. Is that right?

Yes. Your alarm follows the sun, so it moves a little each day as sunrise does. To keep it inside a range, set a wake window ("no earlier than" and "no later than") under Wake time.

What's the difference between Sunrise, Dawn and First light?

  • Sunrise: when the sun breaks the horizon.
  • Dawn: about 25–35 minutes before sunrise, when it's light enough to see outside.
  • First light: the first trace of light, roughly 90 minutes before sunrise.

I'm travelling. Will my alarm follow me?

If your location is set to follow your device, the app updates it each time you open the app. To have it update even when you don't open the app, turn on Update alarms when I travel in Settings › Location and allow "Always" location access.

Where there's no sunrise (polar day or night), what happens?

Turn on the wake window. On days without a sunrise, the alarm rings at the latest time in your window.

How do I see my sleep from Apple Health?

Turn on Health in Settings and allow Sleep access. Last night's sleep then appears on the Home screen.

How do I delete my data?

Use Reset all settings in Settings, or delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else. See the privacy policy.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later. The app is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. An Apple Watch is optional.

Does it work with Apple Watch?

Yes. When the alarm rings it shows on your Apple Watch as well as your iPhone. The Watch app shows your next alarm, today's sunrise and sunset and the next seven mornings, and can skip the next alarm. There is also a complication for your next alarm and the sunrise it follows. Open Rise and Shine on your iPhone once and your alarm appears on the watch.

How do I skip a morning, or ring at a set time just once?

On the Home screen, tap the skip button beside that morning; tap it again to restore it. For a one-off time, such as an early flight, touch and hold the morning and choose Set a custom time. It applies to that morning only, and the sunrise rule takes over again the next day. To stop alarms for a holiday, turn on Pause alarms until a date under Days.

Can weekends follow a different rule?

Yes. Open Wake time from the Home screen and turn on Different rule on Saturday and Sunday. Weekends then get their own anchor, offset and wake window, starting an hour later than your weekday rule. Which days ring at all is set under Days.

How long is snooze?

Nine minutes to start with. Change it under Wake time, anywhere from 1 to 30 minutes.
